About The Story

Vagabond is Takehiko Inoue’s acclaimed manga, a retelling of the life of Miyamoto Musashi, Japan’s legendary swordsman. It begins in 1600 with Takezo, a wild young man who survives a lost battle and is later reshaped by a monk into Musashi, who sets out to become the greatest swordsman alive. As he travels and duels famous fighters, including his destined rival Sasaki Kojiro, he starts to question what true strength means and what a life of violence costs. It’s known for stunning artwork and a thoughtful, philosophical tone, though it has been on hiatus since 2015 and remains unfinished.

Vagabond is available to be read in three different ways. Originally there was the Single Tankōbon Volumes which contained a total of 37 volumes, however these are no longer sold officially and can only be found on online marketplaces such as ebay. There are also the newer and hardcover Definitive/Deluxe Editions that are still ongoing in release. And finally, the most common and affordable way to read is the VIZBIG Editions which act as omnibus’ and adapt 3 standard volumes each.
